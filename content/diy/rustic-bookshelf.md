## Project Overview

I decided to build a rustic wooden bookshelf to display my book collection and add some character to my study. This project took about two weeks to complete, including design, cutting, finishing, and installation.

## Materials Used

- Reclaimed pine wood from an old barn (for the shelves)
- Black metal brackets for support
- Natural wood stain and polyurethane finish
- Various screws, nails, and wall anchors

## Design & Planning

The bookshelf is designed with three shelves, each measuring 36 inches wide and 10 inches deep. The rustic aesthetic comes from:
- Natural wood grain and knots
- Weathered appearance from the reclaimed timber
- Industrial metal brackets for contrast
- Open design without a back panel

## Construction Process

### Preparation
First, I prepared the reclaimed wood by sanding it down and removing surface debris. The wood had some beautiful patina that I wanted to preserve.

### Cutting & Assembly
- Cut the wood to the required dimensions
- Sanded all edges smooth
- Applied stain and polyurethane finish
- Attached metal brackets to the underside of each shelf

### Installation
- Located studs in the wall
- Used heavy-duty anchors for extra support
- Mounted brackets securely
- Tested weight capacity before adding books

## Challenges & Solutions

**Challenge 1:** The reclaimed wood was warped in places.
**Solution:** I carefully selected the straightest pieces and used wood conditioner to even out some of the warping.

**Challenge 2:** Getting the shelves perfectly level.
**Solution:** Used a laser level and made minor adjustments with shims during installation.

## Final Result

The bookshelf turned out beautifully! It adds warmth to the room and showcases my book collection well. The open design makes it easy to display both books and decorative objects.

## Lessons Learned

- Reclaimed wood requires more prep work but adds unique character
- Taking time to plan and measure properly prevents mistakes
- The right tools and hardware make installation much easier
- A simple design can be very effective
