## Coffee Shop Observations

Spent a quiet afternoon at my favorite local coffee shop, capturing quick sketches of the space and people. There's something special about drawing in cafés – the ambient noise, the presence of other people, and the endless stream of interesting faces to sketch.

## What I Focused On

- The interplay of light coming through the large windows
- People engaged in their own work and conversations
- The cozy feeling created by warm wood tones and vintage décor
- Details of coffee cups and the ritual of the space

## Techniques Used

- Ballpoint pen for quick linework
- Watercolor washes to add atmosphere
- Quick gesture sketches of people

This sketch reminds me to take time for observation and to find inspiration in everyday moments.
