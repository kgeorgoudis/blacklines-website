## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: DevOps section displays technical posts
The DevOps section SHALL display blog posts about DevOps topics, infrastructure, and related technical content.

#### Scenario: User browses DevOps posts
- **WHEN** user navigates to the DevOps section
- **THEN** a list of blog posts is displayed

### Requirement: DevOps posts include content and metadata
Each DevOps post entry SHALL include a title, publication date, and content summary or excerpt.

#### Scenario: User reads post information
- **WHEN** user views a DevOps post entry
- **THEN** title, date, and summary/excerpt are visible

### Requirement: DevOps posts support full article content
Posts in the DevOps section SHALL support viewing full article content.

#### Scenario: User reads full DevOps article
- **WHEN** user clicks on a DevOps post
- **THEN** the full article content is displayed

### Requirement: DevOps posts are organized chronologically
Posts in the DevOps section SHALL be ordered by publication date with newest first.

#### Scenario: User sees posts in reverse chronological order
- **WHEN** user views the DevOps section
- **THEN** posts are displayed with most recent first
